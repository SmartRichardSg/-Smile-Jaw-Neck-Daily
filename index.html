⚠️ Important disclaimer

This app provides general exercise guidance only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Stop immediately if you feel pain, increasing discomfort, dizziness, headache, numbness, tingling, weakness, jaw locking/clicking that is painful, or any unusual symptom. Do not force a movement or stretch.

If symptoms persist or you have a medical condition, recent injury, significant neck/jaw problem, or are unsure whether these exercises are suitable for you, consult a qualified healthcare professional before continuing.

Perform all exercises gently, within a comfortable range, and breathe normally.
